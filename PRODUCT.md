## Platform

web

## Users

The primary early audience is solo book resellers evaluating a future platform for sourcing, inventory, listings, repricing, orders, reporting, and fulfillment. They need to understand the product quickly and decide whether to join its early-access list.

## Product Purpose

Rebound is a lifecycle-driven book commerce operations platform. It is intended to connect the work behind a physical book—from its first scan and sourcing decision through inventory, marketplace listing, pricing, sale, and fulfillment.

The public website explains that product, builds trust before the application is available, and converts interested resellers into early-access subscribers.

## Positioning

Rebound follows each physical copy through one connected operating lifecycle instead of splitting scanning, profitability research, inventory, marketplace listings, repricing, and reporting across disconnected tools.

## Operating Context

The website is a prelaunch SaaS marketing experience. Real application screenshots are not available yet, so conceptual product demonstrations must be clearly labeled as previews or sample data. The early-access form is the primary conversion. A separate login route communicates that product accounts are not yet available.

## Product Principles

1. Make the connected lifecycle understandable within the first visit.
2. Show how the product works without pretending unfinished capabilities already exist.
3. Give the early-access action clear priority across every route.
4. Use product-specific book-reselling language and evidence instead of generic SaaS claims.
5. Make the brand memorable without compromising reading, accessibility, or trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, reduced motion, meaningful headings and landmarks, adequate touch targets, zoom and reflow, and accessible form feedback.
